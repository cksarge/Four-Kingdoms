W A S D Move
Mouse Aim
LMB Attack (hold to chain)
RMB Block with shield
LMB With a bow: hold to draw, release to shoot. A full draw flies farthest and hits hardest
R Switch arrow type (bows are two-handed: no blocking)
Tab Swap to your sidearm (set one in the inventory)
C Special move: sword whirlwind, axe crushing blow, spear lunge, piercing arrow; while blocking, a shield bash
Space Dodge: time it just as a blow lands to take no damage
E Talk / loot / interact
F Mount, dismount, call horse
Shift Sprint (hold; drains stamina)
Q+LMB Hit friendly or neutral characters (villagers, guards, animals). Hurting people is a crime
P Skills (one point per level)
G Companions: follow or hold position
I Inventory
M Map
K Diplomacy (kingdoms)
J Quest journal
1–3 Eat food / drink potion
Esc Pause / close

You begin as a poor hedge knight near a village of Albion. Explore the continent, hunt bandits, complete quests, trade goods between kingdoms, and build your reputation with four rival crowns. Your deeds shift the balance between the realms: help one kingdom and its rivals will take notice.

Bandit camps hide in forests, hills and mountains; harder camps guard better loot. Kingdom soldiers turn hostile if your reputation falls too low, or if you swear allegiance to their enemy. Villages offer work, rest, rumors and trade. Rulers grant audiences in their castles.
