You begin as a poor hedge knight near a village of Albion. Explore the continent, hunt bandits, complete quests, trade goods between kingdoms, and build your reputation with four rival crowns. Your deeds shift the balance between the realms: help one kingdom and its rivals will take notice.

Bandit camps hide in forests, hills and mountains; harder camps guard better loot. Kingdom soldiers turn hostile if your reputation falls too low, or if you swear allegiance to their enemy. Villages offer work, rest, rumors and trade. Rulers grant audiences in their castles.
